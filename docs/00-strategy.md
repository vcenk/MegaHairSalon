# 00 — Strategy

## Positioning Statement

> Megas Hair Salon brings four decades of Turkish hair artistry to the Tri-Cities — master colourists specializing in balayage, blonde transformations, and precision cuts in the heart of Coquitlam.

## Why this works

- **"Coquitlam" not "Vancouver"** — Vancouver SERPs are saturated (Suki's, Civello, 50+ established players). Coquitlam + Tri-Cities has weaker competition and stronger purchase intent ("I need a salon near me").
- **"Turkish" is a moat** — almost no salon in Greater Vancouver owns this term. Authentic to your team. Captures the Turkish/Middle Eastern diaspora searches.
- **"Master colourists since 1984"** — heritage is rare in this market. Most competitors opened post-2010. Lean into it.

## Target Audience

### Primary: Tri-Cities women, 30–55, household income $90k+
- Lives or works in Coquitlam, Port Moody, Port Coquitlam, Burquitlam
- Already pays $200+ for color; values consistency and skill over price
- Disappointed by chain salons and inexperienced stylists
- Searches: "best balayage Coquitlam", "blonde specialist near me", "experienced colourist Tri-Cities"

### Secondary: Turkish/Middle Eastern community across Greater Vancouver
- Will drive 30–60 minutes for cultural fit + language comfort
- Searches: "Turkish hairdresser Vancouver", "kuaför Vancouver"
- Smaller volume, very high loyalty and word-of-mouth

### Tertiary: Men seeking premium grooming
- Coquitlam professionals, 35–60
- Searches: "men's haircut Coquitlam", "barber Pacific St"
- Currently underserved on the existing site

## Brand Voice

- **Confident, not boastful** — "Master colourists" not "Best in Coquitlam"
- **Warm, not casual** — Turkish hospitality translates to genuine care, not chumminess
- **Editorial, not promotional** — Vogue tone, not Groupon tone
- **Bilingual touchpoints** — occasional Turkish phrases (e.g. "Hoş geldiniz" in footer) signal authenticity without alienating

## Competitor Landscape (Coquitlam)

| Salon | Strength | Weakness to exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Filomena Salon & Spa | Strong local rankings, 15+ years | Generic visual brand |
| Vancut | "Blonde specialist" SEO position | Single stylist focus |
| Remix Hair Studio | Near Coquitlam Centre, walk-ins | No story, dated site |
| Haute Hair Coquitlam | 20+ years, premium positioning | Weak content depth |
| Ciel Hair Salon | Yelp top-3 | Limited online presence |

**Megas's edge:** 1984 heritage + Turkish artistry + 3 master stylists + better photography + faster site = differentiated premium.

## Success Metrics (12 months post-launch)

- Top 3 Google Maps for "hair salon Coquitlam"
- Top 5 organic for "balayage Coquitlam", "blonde specialist Coquitlam"
- #1 organic for "Turkish hair salon Vancouver"
- 50+ new Google reviews (currently 146)
- Core Web Vitals: all green on mobile
- Online bookings: 60%+ of new client acquisition
