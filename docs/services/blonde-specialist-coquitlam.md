### 8. FAQ

**How long does going blonde take at Megas?**
A full blonde appointment usually takes 3–5 hours. If you're going significantly lighter or correcting previous colour, the appointment may need to be split across two visits to protect your hair.

**Can I go blonde if my hair has been box-coloured?**
Often yes, but the process is more involved and may take multiple sessions. We'll tell you honestly what's achievable at the consultation. Never trust a salon that promises platinum from box dye in one sitting.

**Will going blonde damage my hair?**
Lightening always involves some structural change. Our job is to minimize it. We use bond protectors during lightening, finish every appointment with a Kérastase treatment, and recommend home care that maintains hair health between visits.

**How often should I touch up my blonde?**
Bright blondes typically need a root touch-up every 4–6 weeks. Lived-in blondes and balayage extend that to 8–14 weeks. Toner refreshes between appointments keep colour from fading or pulling brassy.

**What's the price range for going blonde at Megas?**
Most blonde services fall between $280 and $450. Color correction work — for previous bad colour, deep box dye, or significant transformations — starts at $500. We confirm pricing at consultation.
