### 5. FAQ

**How long does a blow dry take at Megas?**
A standard blow dry takes 30–45 minutes. Special occasion styling takes 60–90 minutes depending on complexity. Bridal styling is booked as a longer appointment.

**Should I come with clean or dirty hair?**
Either works — our blow dry service starts with a wash. If you'd like to skip the wash for a quick refresh, let us know at booking and we'll adjust the time and price.

**Can you do my hair for my wedding?**
Yes. We offer bridal trials and on-the-day styling. Book the trial 4–6 weeks before your wedding so we have time to perfect the look. We can also accommodate bridal parties.

**How long does a blowout last?**
Most blowouts hold for 3–4 days with proper care. Sleep on a silk pillowcase, avoid touching your hair, and use dry shampoo at the roots to extend it. Emir is known for blowouts that hold up to 5 days.

**Do you offer mobile or in-home styling?**
Currently we serve clients in-salon only. We can accommodate early morning bridal appointments by request — please call to arrange.
