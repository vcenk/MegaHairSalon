### 5. FAQ

**How much does a haircut cost at Megas Coquitlam?**
Women's cuts start at $85, men's at $55, children at $45. A wash, cut, and blow dry is $110. Final pricing depends on length and complexity — confirmed before we start.

**Do I need to book in advance?**
We strongly recommend booking ahead, especially for weekends. Walk-ins are welcome when our stylists have availability, but Saturday slots typically book a week or more in advance.

**Can you cut curly or textured hair?**
Yes. Our master stylists work with every hair type — straight, wavy, curly, coily, fine, and coarse. We cut dry or wet depending on what your texture needs.

**What's included in a haircut appointment?**
Consultation, shampoo, conditioning rinse, the cut itself, and a finishing blow dry and style. We finish with product recommendations to maintain the cut at home.

**How often should I get a haircut?**
Most clients return every 6–10 weeks for cuts. Shorter cuts and styles with precise lines need more frequent visits; long layered cuts can stretch to 12 weeks. We'll suggest a schedule based on your cut.
