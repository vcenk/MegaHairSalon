Our story

From a Gaziantep kitchen to East Hastings.

Baklava isn't a dessert you rush. In our family it was a Sunday ritual — phyllo pulled until you could read a newspaper through it, pistachios ground the same morning, syrup poured while the pastry was still whispering out of the oven.

Emilee's Sweet House brings that exact ritual to Vancouver. No shortcuts, no day-old trays, no imitation nuts. Just the sweets we grew up on, made the honest way, for a neighbourhood that's become family.

The craft

What goes into one piece

Ordinary baklava has a handful of thick layers. Ours has forty — and every one is brushed by hand.

  1. 1

    Hand-pulled phyllo

    Dough rested and stretched paper-thin, layer over layer, brushed with sweet butter.

  2. 2

    Antep pistachios

    Deep-green pistachios from Gaziantep, ground fresh the morning they're baked.

  3. 3

    Slow bake

    Baked until the top shatters like glass, then rested to set the crunch.

  4. 4

    Warm honey syrup

    Light lemon-honey syrup poured the second it leaves the oven — never soggy, always crisp.
