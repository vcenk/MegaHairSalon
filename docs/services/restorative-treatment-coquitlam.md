### 5. FAQ

**How do I know if my hair needs a restorative treatment?**
Signs include breakage, mid-shaft snapping, gummy or stretchy strands when wet, dullness that doesn't respond to home care, or hair that won't hold colour. We assess at consultation and recommend honestly.

**How often should I get a treatment?**
Most clients benefit from a treatment every 4–6 weeks alongside their regular services. For severely damaged hair, a more intensive plan over 2–3 months can rebuild structure significantly.

**Will one treatment fix my hair?**
One treatment will help — sometimes dramatically — but real recovery takes consistency. Damaged hair regrows; the goal is to maintain the existing length while it grows out healthier.

**What's the difference between a deep conditioner and a bond rebuilder?**
Deep conditioners moisturize and smooth the surface. Bond rebuilders work inside the hair shaft to repair the broken disulfide bonds caused by chemical processing. Most damaged hair benefits from both.

**Can I add a treatment to another appointment?**
Yes. Adding a treatment to a colour, cut, or blowout is one of the most efficient ways to maintain hair health. Add-on treatments start at $50.
